## Payment roadmap

The current public support page is a voluntary-donation mechanism. Product purchases and recurring subscriptions should be added later through a non-custodial checkout layer such as BTCPay Server, with payment confirmation handled server-side.

Until that backend exists, do **not** claim that a payment unlocks premium functionality automatically. The current support flow is simply voluntary support.

## Safety

Users should verify the selected asset and network in their own wallet before sending. ClimateStudy cannot reverse blockchain transactions.
